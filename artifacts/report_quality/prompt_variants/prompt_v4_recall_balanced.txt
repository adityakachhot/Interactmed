Status: [finding_observed | not_identified | indeterminate | not_assessable]

5. MEDIASTINUM & HILA:
Status: [finding_observed | not_identified | indeterminate | not_assessable]

Status: [finding_observed | not_identified | indeterminate | not_assessable]

OVERALL CLINICAL IMPRESSION:
Summarize all observed abnormalities, devices, and significant negative findings.
- DO NOT default to a "Normal chest radiograph" if any category is "finding_observed" or "indeterminate".

- Preserve uncertainty; never describe an unassessable or indeterminate finding as absent.
