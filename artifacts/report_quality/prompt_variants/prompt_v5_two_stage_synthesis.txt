Provide a clear, cohesive summary of all positive findings, observed devices, and significant negatives:

- DO NOT convert "indeterminate" or "not_assessable" into definitive negative statements.

UNCERTAINTY AND LIMITATIONS:
State technical limitations (single frontal projection, AP magnification, inspiratory depth, motion) as indicated in the extraction.
