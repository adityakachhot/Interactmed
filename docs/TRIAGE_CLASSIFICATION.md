## 1. Executive Summary & Clinical Governance

The InteractMed-VLM framework has been extended to provide **two distinct, decoupled outputs** for each uploaded chest radiograph (CXR):
1. **Full-Image Whole-Radiograph Analysis:** An AI-generated radiology report draft or patient-friendly plain-language explanation (powered by MedGemma 4B).
2. **Research-Only Triage-Priority Suggestion:** A transparent, rule-driven triage priority suggestion supported by structured anatomical findings, explicit rule rationale, uncertainty level, and human-review priority.

### Strict Governance Constraints
- **Zero Clinical Claims:** Neither output is clinically validated, clearance-approved, or intended to establish medical diagnosis or emergency disposition.
- **Rule Engine, Not a Learned Classifier:** Triage assignment is governed by a deterministic, transparent rule engine (`src/triage_service.py`, `configs/triage_rules.yaml`) operating on structured findings. **No machine learning classifier has been trained or evaluated for triage**, and no fabricated accuracy or AUC figures are reported.
- **Strict Separation of Concerns:** Triage logic is strictly separated from report text generation and TransVG-AGPT bounding box grounding.
- **Preservation of Core Pipelines:** The existing locked 117-case expanded test cohort, historical benchmark metrics, TransVG-AGPT grounding weights, and MedGemma LoRA parameter-efficient training scripts remain entirely untouched and decoupled.

## 2. Phase 1 — Design of the Triage Task

### 2.1 Distinguishing Reported Abnormalities from Clinical Triage Urgency
A critical pitfall in AI radiology research is conflating *radiological findings* with *triage urgency*:
- **Radiological Abnormality:** An anatomical or pathological observation (e.g., mild bibasilar atelectasis, stable cardiomegaly, calcified granuloma, sternotomy wires).
- **Clinical Triage Urgency:** An operational clinical determination regarding the necessary speed of physician evaluation to prevent patient harm (e.g., tension pneumothorax, large acute pneumothorax, pulmonary edema with acute compromise).

Existing datasets (MIMIC-CXR, Chest-ImaGenome, MS-CXR, RSNA Pneumonia) label abnormalities or anatomical bounding boxes, but **do not provide operational clinical urgency ground truth**. Labeling all abnormalities as "urgent" results in massive false-alarm rates, clinician alert fatigue, and triage queue disruption.

### 2.2 Four-Category Provisional Triage Schema
InteractMed-VLM establishes a four-category schema designed for clinical safety and transparent review:

| Provisional Category | Operational Meaning | Review Priority | Safety Contract |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `potentially_urgent` | Predefined serious finding flagged (e.g. pneumothorax, severe edema); rapid human review warranted. | `urgent` | **Does not establish an emergency diagnosis.** Flags cases for priority radiologist queue placement. |
| `abnormality_flagged` | Possible non-critical abnormality present (e.g. cardiomegaly, atelectasis, pleural thickening, support devices). | `routine` | Does not differentiate acute from chronic status without prior comparison radiographs. |
| `no_priority_finding_flagged` | Predefined priority patterns were not flagged in the structured findings. | `standard_review` | **MANDATORY CONTRACT: MUST NOT BE INTERPRETED AS NORMAL, HEALTHY, OR DISEASE-FREE.** Routine radiologist workflow required. |
| `indeterminate_review_required` | Insufficient evidence, technically unassessable radiograph, failed model inference, or contradictory outputs. | `urgent` / `routine` | **Safety fail-safe.** Prevents silent false-negative declarations when models fail or contradict themselves. |

### 2.3 Required Output Contract
Every triage output generated by `TriageService` implements a strict schema:
- `triage_category`: One of the 4 defined categories above.
- `matched_rule_id`: Identifier of the rule triggering the category (e.g. `RULE_POTENTIALLY_URGENT_PNEUMOTHORAX`).
- `rule_reason`: Concise human-readable explanation of why the category was assigned.
- `supporting_findings`: List of extracted structured anatomical observations supporting the assignment.
- `uncertainty_level`: Categorical uncertainty (`low`, `medium`, `high`).
- `evidence_notes`: Clarifying observations regarding projection, lateral absence, or image quality.
- `requires_human_review`: Boolean (`true` across all categories).
- `human_review_priority`: `urgent`, `routine`, or `standard_review`.
- `rule_engine_version`: Active engine version (e.g. `1.0.0`).
- `research_disclaimer`: Mandatory warning prohibiting direct clinical reliance.

## 3. Phase 2 — Architecture of the Triage Service

### 3.1 Service Decoupling
The triage service is implemented in [`src/triage_service.py`](file:///Users/adityakachhot/interactmed/src/triage_service.py) as an independent singleton service (`TriageService`). It does not invoke language generation or TransVG tensor operations; instead, it consumes structured findings produced during radiograph analysis.

```
Upload Radiograph (Frontal CXR)
          │
          ├──► TransVG-AGPT (Region Grounding / Bounding Box) [Independent]
          │
          ├──► MedGemma 4B via Ollama (Whole-Image Prompt v3)
          │         │
          │         └──► Structured Findings & Text Synthesis
          │                   │
          │                   ├──► Full Report / Patient Explanation
          │                   │
          │                   └──► Triage Rule Engine (configs/triage_rules.yaml)
          │                             │
          │                             └──► Triage Priority Suggestion
```

### 3.2 Rule Engine Configuration (`configs/triage_rules.yaml`)
Rules are stored externally in [`configs/triage_rules.yaml`](file:///Users/adityakachhot/interactmed/configs/triage_rules.yaml) under semantic sections:
1. `indeterminate_rules`: Evaluated with highest precedence (failed inference, invalid images, severe motion/underexposure, internal contradictions).
2. `urgent_rules`: Evaluated next (pneumothorax, tension cues, acute pulmonary edema).
3. `abnormal_rules`: Evaluated next (cardiomegaly, atelectasis, focal opacity/consolidation, pleural effusion, support apparatus).
4. `default_rule`: `RULE_NO_PRIORITY_FINDINGS_FLAGGED` evaluated when no positive patterns match.

### 3.3 Clinical Negation and Contradiction Safety
The rule engine enforces clinical safety through two specialized mechanisms:
1. **Negation Filtering:** Pre-negation cues (`no `, `without `, `negative for `, `free of `, `cleared of `) and post-negation cues (` unremarkable`, ` ruled out`, ` is absent`) are verified across bounding spans. For example, *"pleural spaces: no pneumothorax"* matches negation and will **never** trigger `potentially_urgent`.
2. **Contradiction Detection:** If observations describe focal pathology (e.g. *"consolidation in right lower lobe"*) but the impression claims *"normal chest radiograph"*, the engine catches the discrepancy and assigns `indeterminate_review_required` rather than suppressing human review.
3. **Failure State Enforcement:** If the vision model times out, fails, or an unparseable image is supplied, the service immediately returns `indeterminate_review_required` with `uncertainty_level: high`.

### 3.4 Patient Privacy & De-Identification
The triage service stores only anonymized rule IDs, normalized category identifiers, and structured anatomical observations. Patient names, birth dates, and unvetted free-text demographics are stripped before triage processing.

## 5. Phase 4 — Triage Evaluation Data Preparation & Human Annotation Protocol

### 5.1 Audit of Existing Dataset Ground Truth
An audit of available local datasets confirms that **none contain validated operational triage urgency annotations**:
- **Chest-ImaGenome:** Contains 26 anatomical bounding boxes and relationship sentences (e.g. "lung opacity located at left lower lobe"). These describe findings, not clinical urgency.
- **MS-CXR:** Contains bounding boxes for 8 clinical findings, but does not provide clinical priority or triage triage labels.
- **MIMIC-CXR Reports:** Contain radiologist free-text impressions, which describe observations but do not reflect institutional triage protocols.
- **RSNA Pneumonia:** Contains binary opacity annotations.

**Conclusion:** Treating finding labels as ground-truth triage urgency would introduce severe label noise and clinical misrepresentation.

### 5.2 Reviewer Annotation Protocol & Template
To prepare true ground truth, an annotation protocol and CSV template have been established in [`templates/triage_annotation_template.csv`](file:///Users/adityakachhot/interactmed/templates/triage_annotation_template.csv).

#### Reviewer Requirements
- **Qualifications:** Board-certified radiologists or senior radiology trainees under faculty supervision.
- **Double-Reading:** Every CXR is independently annotated by two blinded reviewers (`reviewer_1`, `reviewer_2`).
- **Disagreement Adjudication:** Disagreements (`disagreement_flag == TRUE`) are resolved by a designated senior adjudicator (`adjudicator_id`).
- **Image Adequacy Assessment:** Every case includes technical quality logging (`adequate`, `limited_penetration`, `motion_blur`, `unassessable`).

#### Annotation Template Schema
```csv
case_id,patient_id,study_id,split_assignment,technical_adequacy,reference_findings,reviewer_1_id,reviewer_1_category,reviewer_1_critical_finding,reviewer_1_confidence,reviewer_2_id,reviewer_2_category,reviewer_2_critical_finding,reviewer_2_confidence,disagreement_flag,adjudicator_id,adjudicated_category,adjudication_rationale,clinical_policy_version,annotation_timestamp_utc
```

### 5.3 Partitioning Plan & Protection of Locked Cohorts
- **Locked Test Set:** The 117-case patient-disjoint `expanded_test` cohort remains strictly locked and untouched for report-generation benchmarks. It must not be re-used for triage rule tuning or triage classifier training.
- **Proposed Triage Partitions:**
  - `triage_dev` (approx. 60% of newly annotated cases): Rule tuning, pattern validation.
  - `triage_val` (approx. 20%): Hyperparameter and threshold selection.
  - `triage_test` (approx. 20%): Held-out evaluation of triage concordance against adjudicated radiologist consensus.
  - **Constraint:** All splits must be partitioned strictly at the **patient level** (zero patient overlap across splits).

## 6. Phase 5 — Separation from MedGemma LoRA Report Fine-Tuning

- **Independent Pipelines:** The MedGemma LoRA parameter-efficient fine-tuning pilot (`scripts/train_medgemma_lora.py`, `configs/medgemma_finetune.yaml`) is maintained as a report-generation research initiative.
- **No Joint Multi-Task Training:** Triage classification is not combined into a joint objective with report generation. Combining these tasks without verified multi-task annotations risks catastrophic forgetting, finding hallucination, and compromised clinical attribution.
- **Model Checkpoints:** LoRA fine-tuning targets the official Hugging Face multimodal checkpoint (`google/medgemma-4b-it`) in 4-bit QLoRA, strictly avoiding unsupported direct training on quantized Ollama GGUF files.

### 7.3 Remaining Limitations
1. **Rule Engine Coverage:** The current rule dictionary covers primary emergent and common thoracic findings (pneumothorax, edema, cardiomegaly, atelectasis, devices, consolidation), but does not yet encompass rare abnormalities (e.g. pneumomediastinum, diaphragmatic hernia, rib fractures).
2. **Lack of Clinical Urgency Labels:** Until radiologist annotations are performed using [`templates/triage_annotation_template.csv`](file:///Users/adityakachhot/interactmed/templates/triage_annotation_template.csv), triage sensitivity, specificity, and Cohen's kappa cannot be computed.
3. **Absence of Lateral Views:** Single frontal CXRs have inherent technical limitations in retrocardiac and costophrenic evaluation; findings must always note this uncertainty.

### 7.4 Required Steps Prior to Evaluating Triage Classification Performance
Before any quantitative performance evaluation (e.g. sensitivity, specificity, macro-F1, AUROC) can be conducted:
1. Complete dual radiologist reading of candidate cases using `templates/triage_annotation_template.csv`.
2. Adjudicate all inter-annotator disagreements using a third senior radiologist.
3. Establish an institutionally agreed clinical urgency policy.
4. Confirm zero patient overlap between `triage_dev`, `triage_val`, and `triage_test`.
5. Maintain strict data logging separating model predictions from human labels.
